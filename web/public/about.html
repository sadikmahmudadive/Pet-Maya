About Pet Maya & VertexHand

Transforming Daily Pet Care Through Smart Innovation

We are on a mission to empower pet parents, veterinary doctors, and pet service providers with a unified, intelligent mobile healthcare ecosystem.

🐾 Our Story & Mission

Pet Maya was founded with a single driving principle: pet care should be seamless, proactive, and compassionate. Pets are family, yet tracking immunizations, deciphering health symptoms, and connecting with certified veterinarians often remains fragmented and stressful.

Developed by VertexHand & MASA, Pet Maya merges cutting-edge mobile engineering with veterinary best practices, giving pet parents real-time diagnostic tools, verified doctor bookings, and a safe, engaging community.

✨ Core Platform Pillars

🩺 Certified Vet Teleconsults

Instantly find and book verified veterinarians in your area for in-clinic visits or remote video consultations.

🤖 AI Health Symptom Scanner

Analyze pet symptoms and skin abnormalities in seconds with smart triage assistance and urgency classification.

📅 Smart Vaccine & Meal Planner

Never miss a booster shot or meal with proactive multi-channel alarms, push alerts, and calendar sync.

🐾 Verified Pet Community

Share adorable moments, connect with nearby pet owners, discover reputable pet shops, and adopt safely.

🔒 Data Privacy & Google Authentication

We believe that your trust and your pet's health information must be protected with the highest industry standards.

Why We Use Google Sign-In:
We use Google OAuth 2.0 to provide frictionless, enterprise-grade authentication. Your Google account securely identifies you across devices, preserves your pet's medical histories, and enables 1-tap calendar synchronization for upcoming veterinary appointments.

We never sell or monetize your personal data. All medical records, photos, and messages are encrypted in transit and at rest via Firebase Cloud Architecture.
