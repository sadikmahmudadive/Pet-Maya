Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about Pet Maya? Here are the most common inquiries from our community.

How are veterinarians verified on Pet Maya?
Every veterinarian on Pet Maya undergoes medical board license verification, clinic certification review, and identity validation before being published.
Is the symptom checker free to use?
Yes! The smart health triage checker is completely free for all pet owners to perform preliminary health assessments and preventive care checks.
Can I access my pet records without internet?
Yes! Pet Maya uses an offline-first architecture. All your saved pet health IDs, vaccination schedules, and prescription histories remain accessible even when offline.
How do I delete my account or remove my data?
You can delete your account directly inside the app (Profile → Edit Profile → Delete Account) or by emailing siradive137@gmail.com. All data is permanently purged within 30 days.
Transparency & Trust

Your Privacy Is Our Priority

Pet Maya is built on a foundation of transparency. Here is exactly what data we request, why we need it, and how it protects your pet.

Google Account (Sign-In)

We use Google Sign-In via Firebase Authentication to create and secure your account. We access your name, email address, and profile photo only to identify you within the app.

Purpose: Secure account creation & login

Location Access

High-accuracy location is used solely to display nearby certified veterinary clinics on our interactive map. Location data is processed in real-time and never stored on our servers after your session ends.

Purpose: Find nearby vets & clinics

Camera & Photos

When you use the Health Scanner, photos of your pet are uploaded securely for diagnostic analysis. These images are used solely to provide diagnostic insights and are never shared with advertisers.

Purpose: Health symptom analysis

Push Notifications

We send timely reminders for upcoming veterinary appointments, vaccination schedules, and medicine doses. You can manage or disable notifications at any time from your device settings.

Purpose: Health reminders & alerts

Pet Profile Data

Pet name, breed, age, weight, and medical history are stored securely in Firebase Firestore with AES-256 encryption. This data powers your pet's health dashboard, vaccine tracker, and vet appointment history.

Purpose: Pet health management

What We Never Do

We never sell your personal data. We never use your information for advertising profiling. We never share your pet's medical records with third parties without your explicit consent.
