Transparency & Trust

Your Privacy Is Our Priority

Pet Maya is built on a foundation of transparency. Here is exactly what data we request, why we need it, and how it protects your pet.

Google Account (Sign-In)

We use Google Sign-In via Firebase Authentication to create and secure your account. We access your name, email, and avatar only to identify you within the app.

Purpose: Secure account authentication

Location Access

Used solely to display nearby certified veterinary clinics on our interactive map. Location data is never shared with third parties.

Purpose: Find nearby vets

Camera & Pet Photos

When you use the AI Health Scanner, photos of your pet are uploaded securely for diagnostic analysis and are never sold or used for advertising.

Purpose: AI symptom analysis

Push Notifications

We send timely reminders for upcoming veterinary appointments, vaccination schedules, and medicine doses.

Purpose: Health reminders & alarms

Pet Profile Data

Pet breed, age, weight, and vaccine records are stored securely in Firebase Firestore with AES-256 encryption.

Purpose: Pet health records

What We Never Do

We never sell your data. We never use personal info for ad profiling. We never share records without your consent.

Our privacy guarantee
